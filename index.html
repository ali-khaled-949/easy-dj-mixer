Getting started

Easy DJ Mixer is landscape-only — turn your iPhone sideways after opening it. A short guide appears the first time you launch. You can skip it, and reopen it any time with the ? button next to the crossfader.

Load a track

Tap + on either deck to open your library, then tap A or B next to a song to load it onto that deck. Two demo tracks are included so you can start immediately.

Import your own music

In the library, tap the import button and pick files from Files or iCloud Drive. MP3, M4A, WAV and AIFF all work. Imported tracks are copied into the app so they stay available offline.

Play, cue and scratch

  • CUE sets your start point. Hold it to preview, release to snap back.
  • Spin the platter to scratch — your finger's speed is the playback speed. Pull backwards to play in reverse.
  • Drag the waveform to nudge a track into place, or tap the overview strip to jump.

Mix the two decks

  • Crossfader at the bottom centre blends deck A into deck B.
  • EQ knobs (HI / MID / LOW) go from full kill to +6 dB.
  • FILTER is low-pass one way, high-pass the other.
  • PITCH matches tempos. Tap ±8 to widen the range to ±16% or ±50%.

Resetting a control

Tap the ↺ arrow beside the A or B label to flatten that deck's EQ and filter instantly. It lights up only when something is off-neutral. To reset a single knob or fader, double-tap it.

Common questions

Can I mix songs from Spotify or Apple Music?

No — and no DJ app can, without a licensing partnership with that service. Streaming services do not allow their audio to be decoded and manipulated by third-party apps, which is what mixing requires.

Easy DJ Mixer plays audio files stored on your device: music you've bought, downloaded, or made yourself.

Why do I have to sign in?

Sign-in with Apple or Google identifies you so your library stays yours on this device. There is no server behind the app — your account details are stored only in your iPhone's keychain, and your music never leaves your phone. See the privacy policy for detail.

How do I sign out or delete my account?

Tap the person icon next to the CROSSFADER label. Sign Out keeps your imported tracks; Delete Account removes your account and every track you've imported from the device.

Why is the app landscape only?

The layout mirrors real DJ equipment — two decks either side of a centre mixer. That doesn't fit a portrait phone at a usable size.

Is the BPM always right?

BPM is estimated automatically when a track is imported. It's accurate for most music with a steady beat, but it's an estimate, not a beat grid — use the pitch fader and your ears to fine-tune.
