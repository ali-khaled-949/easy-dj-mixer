# Privacy Policy — Easy DJ Mixer

**Last updated:** 26 July 2026

## The short version

Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, the app learns who you are.
That is the only personal information involved, it is stored on your own device, and it
is never sent to us — we operate no servers. Your music never leaves your iPhone.

## What we collect

When you sign in, the app receives from Apple or Google:

- a unique account identifier
- your email address
- your name, when the provider supplies it

**This information is stored only in the iOS keychain on your device.** There is no
backend, no database, and no account on our side. We never receive, see, or store it.

The app collects nothing else: no analytics, no advertising identifier, no usage
tracking, no location, no contacts, no crash reporting.

### Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your real email address, and Apple
will supply a private relay address instead. The app works exactly the same either way.
Apple only shares your name and email the first time you authorize, so the app stores
what it is given at that moment.

### Sign in with Google

Choosing Google opens Google's own sign-in page in a secure system browser. Your Google
password is entered on Google's page and is never visible to this app. Google's handling
of that sign-in is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app requests only the `openid`, `email` and `profile` scopes — enough to identify
you and nothing more. It cannot read your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google
data.

## Your music

When you import an audio file, the app copies it into its own private storage on your
iPhone so it stays available. That copy never leaves your device. It is not uploaded,
shared, or analysed anywhere but on your phone.

## Network access

The app connects to the internet only during sign-in, to Apple's and Google's
authentication services. Everything else — playback, mixing, waveform analysis, BPM
detection — happens entirely on your device, offline.

## Signing out and deleting your account

- **Sign Out** (the Account button at the bottom of the centre mixer) removes your
  session from the device. Your imported tracks stay.
- **Delete Account** (same menu) removes your session *and* permanently deletes every
  track you have imported. Because your account exists only on your device, this
  deletes it completely — there is nothing left anywhere else.

You can also revoke the app's access from your provider at any time:
[Apple ID settings](https://appleid.apple.com) or
[Google account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no
third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use the advertising identifier and does
not track you across other apps or websites.

## Children

The app is rated 4+. It collects nothing beyond the sign-in identity described above,
and stores that only on the device.
