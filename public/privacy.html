Privacy Policy

Easy DJ Mixer · Last updated 26 July 2026

The short version. Easy DJ Mixer asks you to sign in with Apple or Google so the app knows who you are. That is the only personal information involved, it is stored on your own device, and it is never sent to us — we operate no servers. Your music never leaves your iPhone.

What we collect

When you sign in, the app receives the following from Apple or Google:

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Account identifierTo recognise you on returnDevice keychain
Email addressTo identify your accountDevice keychain
Name (when provided)To show who is signed inDevice keychain

This information is stored only in the iOS keychain on your device. There is no backend, no database, and no account on our side. We never receive, see, or store it.

The app collects nothing else: no analytics, no advertising identifier, no usage tracking, no location, no contacts, no crash reporting.

Sign in with Apple

You may choose to hide your real email address, and Apple will supply a private relay address instead. The app works exactly the same either way. Apple only shares your name and email the first time you authorise, so the app stores what it is given at that moment.

Sign in with Google

Choosing Google opens Google's own sign-in page in a secure system browser. Your Google password is entered on Google's page and is never visible to this app. Google's handling of that sign-in is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The app requests only the openid, email and profile scopes — enough to identify you and nothing more. It cannot read your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

Your music

When you import an audio file, the app copies it into its own private storage on your iPhone so it stays available. That copy never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, shared, or analysed anywhere but on your phone.

Network access

The app connects to the internet only during sign-in, to Apple's and Google's authentication services. Everything else — playback, mixing, waveform analysis, BPM detection — happens entirely on your device, offline.

Signing out and deleting your account

Both are in the Account menu, reached with the person icon next to the crossfader.

  • Sign Out removes your session from the device. Your imported tracks stay.
  • Delete Account removes your session and permanently deletes every track you have imported. Because your account exists only on your device, this deletes it completely — there is nothing left anywhere else.

You can also revoke the app's access at any time from Apple ID settings or Google account permissions.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It collects nothing beyond the sign-in identity described above, and stores that only on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.
